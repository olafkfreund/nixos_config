# Intent: put p620's three new disks to use

## Problem

On 2026-10-06 three disks were added to p620. None of them is in the
configuration yet:

| Disk | Serial | Health | Contents |
| ---- | ------ | ------ | -------- |
| Fanxiang S501Q 1 TB NVMe | 26060256623000377 | New: 0 h, 0 bytes written | Blank |
| MS4SC2JH2ORA480G 480 GB SATA SSD | S2UBNX0J100050 | 52,786 h, ~54 TB written, wear 99/100 | Blank |
| Micron MTFDDAK256TBN 256 GB SATA SSD | UFZMN01J186ZIF | 435 h, 5 reallocated NAND blocks | Old pfSense ZFS install |

Meanwhile the root NVMe (Kingston, `/` at 68%, 583 GB used) carries
everything that has no disk of its own:

- `/home`, including the large per-user state.
- The nix build dir and both nixarchy runners' VM disks
  (`services.nixarchy-runner.buildDir = "/var/lib/nix-build"`). These were
  moved there as a stopgap for Issue #2156, with runner instances cut from 4 to 2.
- Docker's data root, `/mnt/img_pool/docker` (19 GB), which is a plain
  directory on `/`.

Two older drives also need a decision:

- The old `/mnt/games` Fanxiang S101Q (serial ...30717) is plugged in again on
  port ata9. Issue #2156 found it failing sustained writes on ata4.
- The `/mnt/data` Fanxiang (serial ...30777) missed the port probe at boot
  (ata3 link down) and only appeared after a manual SCSI rescan. That points
  to power or cabling. It could recur after any reboot and silently put
  `/mnt/data` paths back on `/`.

## Proposed outcome

- `/home` lives on the new 1 TB NVMe, its own fast disk, separate from the
  system and the Nix store.
- Build and CI scratch (nix `build-dir`, runner VM disks) and Docker's data
  root live on the 480 GB SSD. With that, the runners go back to 4 instances.
- The root NVMe holds only the OS and `/nix/store`, with room to grow.
- Every new mount is declared by UUID in `hosts/p620/nixos/hardware-configuration.nix`,
  and a missing disk fails visibly instead of quietly writing to `/`.
- The pfSense disk and the suspect `/mnt/games` drive have a recorded fate.

## Affected users and systems

- p620 only: `hosts/p620/nixos/hardware-configuration.nix`,
  `hosts/p620/configuration.nix` (nixarchy-runner, docker dataRoot).
- The user's whole home directory, including the syncthing-synced `~/.claude`
  and `~/.gemini`, and running desktop sessions.
- The nixarchy CI runner pool, which p620 shares with p510.
- p510 and razer are untouched.

## Constraints

- Moving `/home` means copying it while nobody is logged in (from a TTY or a
  rescue boot). A copy taken from a live session misses or corrupts open
  state. The old copy stays until the new one is verified.
- The 480 GB SSD must fit the runners' peak (about 64 GB for 2, so more for 4),
  Docker (19 GB) and nix builds. That needs measuring before raising instances.
- Both `nix.settings.build-dir` and the nix-daemon `TMPDIR` must move
  together; the runner module already does this.
- Docker's `HostConfig` freezes paths. Moving the data root means
  recreating containers, not restarting them.
- Wiping the Micron destroys the pfSense install. That needs the user's
  explicit go-ahead.
- Announce on the agent bus before the `/home` cutover and the deploy.
- No auto-discovery and no inline services: everything goes through the
  existing modules.
