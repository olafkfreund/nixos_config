# Intent: a fast local MoE model for razer

## Problem

razer has no usable local LLM. Ollama is off there (`aiDefaults.profile =
"laptop"`, `hosts/razer/configuration.nix:135`), and its 8 GB RTX 3080 Laptop
can't hold a capable dense model. A 27B dense model runs at under 1 token/s
when it spills into RAM.

The XDA article (2026-10-22) shows the way round it for mixture-of-experts
(MoE) models. llama.cpp's `--n-cpu-moe N` keeps the expert weights in system
RAM and the rest on the GPU. On an RTX 3070 Mobile, the author's very close
match for razer, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B went from about 4 to about 33 tokens/s.
razer has 62 GB of RAM, so the weights fit.

There is already a GPU server for models on the tailnet. p620 runs Ollama
on an RX 7900 XTX (24 GB) through `features.ollama-server`, bound to
`0.0.0.0:11434` for other hosts (`hosts/p620/configuration.nix:131-150`).
A 35B-A3B at IQ4 is about 17 GB, so it fits in that card's VRAM whole, with
no offload trick needed.

## Proposed outcome

A Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-class MoE model answers prompts from razer at a speed
you can read along with (20+ tokens/s). At least one of these is true:

- **Online:** it works over the tailnet with no local GPU or battery cost.
- **Offline:** it works on razer alone, started on demand. It doesn't hold
  about 7 GB of VRAM and 17 GB of RAM while idle.

## Affected users and systems

- razer, if it runs locally: a CUDA `llama-cpp` package, and possibly a new
  binary cache in `flake.nix`
- p620, if it serves: one more model in `features.ollama-server`, about 17 GB
  more on `/mnt/data`. Only one model is loaded at a time
  (`MAX_LOADED_MODELS=1`), so it evicts `qwen3.8:27b` while it is in use.
- `flake.nix` `nixConfig.extra-substituters`, if `cache.nixos-cuda.org` is
  added. That affects every host.

## Constraints

- No always-on service on razer. It's a battery laptop, and a server holding
  the dGPU stops it sleeping.
- **Model weights stay out of the Nix store.** They are 17 GB files,
  downloaded at runtime (by Ollama, or `llama-server -hf`) into a user or
  data directory, never with `fetchurl`.
- **A server that listens is local-only on razer** (`127.0.0.1`). p620's
  existing exposure model doesn't change.
- **The CUDA build comes from a binary cache, not a local compile.** The
  CUDA `llama-cpp` is on `cache.nixos-cuda.org`; the configured
  cuda-maintainers cache misses. Its public key must be looked up, not
  guessed.
- p510 is untouched.

## Decisions (approved 2026-10-24)

1. **(b): p620 serves the model through its existing Ollama**, and razer uses
   it over the tailnet. Nothing changes on razer locally, and there's no new
   cache.
2. **Model:** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at about IQ4_XS. The exact Ollama tag is
   confirmed in the spec.
