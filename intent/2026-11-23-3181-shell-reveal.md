# Intent: login cover lifts through the normal path on p620

Follows the measurement in #3165 (timeline: issue 3165, comment 7094482325).

## Problem

At a p620 login the black startup cover stays up about 12 s after the Omarchy
shell starts, and lifts only because the 10 s safety timer in
`services/BackgroundIntro.qml` fires (`fade-start timeout`). The normal reveal
needs three inputs at once: wallpaper ready, `desktopReady`, and two rendered
frames on each cover. The 2026-11-23 login showed:

- **A second plugin scan.** The first scan ends at +1.5 s, after 0.17 s. A
  second one starts at +5.1 s, right after the last bar widget registers, and
  runs 4.0 s while 16 panels load, with a 2.0 s main-loop stall.
  `desktopReady` is false for the whole scan. Nothing logged names its caller:
  there is no `Local plugin changed` line, and a pending rescan would have
  fired at +1.5 s. That leaves the IPC `rescanPlugins` call and
  `finishPluginReload`.
- **A 2.4 s stall right after that scan** (+9.1 s to +11.5 s), with nothing
  logged. It sits where the post-scan syncs (`_syncServices`,
  `syncPanelEntries`, `syncPluginWidgets`) run. Because of it, the covers
  cannot draw their two frames before the timer.
- **Duplicate creation.** Every bar widget instance is built twice per bar,
  first from the inline `component` and then from the `registry`, and nine
  plugins warn that a second IPC handler was registered. `omarchy.background`
  is created twice (+1.5 s and +13.3 s). Panels are not duplicated.

The wallpaper was ready at +7.8 s, so it did not hold up the reveal.

## Proposed outcome

At a p620 login, the cover lifts through the normal path (`fade-start
normal`) well inside the 10 s safety timer. The target is under 5 s after the
bars map, measured with the #3165 timing patch applied again for one login.
Each change is shown by that log to remove its cost:

1. The second scan's caller is known, and the scan no longer runs during
   startup, or it no longer blocks `desktopReady`.
2. The stall after a scan is gone or much shorter.
3. Each bar widget, and `omarchy.background`, is built once.

## Affected users and systems

- Hosts: p620 for the measurement. razer and p510 run the same Omarchy shell
  through nixarchy, so a source fix reaches them as well. Their logins change
  only by getting faster.
- Code: the Omarchy shell (`shell.qml`, `services/PluginRegistry.qml`,
  `services/BackgroundIntro.qml`) as shipped by nixarchy. Where the cause is
  our wiring (a nixarchy unit or script calling `rescanPlugins` at login),
  the fix belongs in olafkfreund/nixarchy. Where it is in the shell itself, it
  goes upstream to omacom/omarchy, and nixarchy carries a patch until
  upstream releases it.
- This repo: only the nixarchy pin, plus the p620 timing patch for the
  measurement login, applied and reverted the same way as #3179/#3180.

## Constraints

- Prefer removing the cause over hiding it. For example, do not shorten the
  10 s safety timer, and do not drop the two-frame check.
- No change to which plugins load or what they do. A plugin that is added or
  edited while the session runs must still be picked up.
- Measuring the second scan's caller needs one more instrumented login. Keep
  that patch temporary and gated, as in #3165.
- p510 is never built or deployed without the owner's OK. Announce on the
  agent bus before any deploy.
- The owner logs out and in for each measurement login.

## Open questions

- **One task, or one per cause?** Recommendation: this task finds the second
  scan's caller and fixes it first, because it is the largest cost and it
  also sets off the stall after it. Widget double-building and the
  background service's second creation become their own issues, unless the
  same fix removes them.
- **Where does the fix land?** This is decided per cause once the caller is
  known: nixarchy if our wiring triggers the scan, upstream Omarchy if the
  shell does.
