# Intent: p620 switches itself after a green edge, without killing CI jobs

## Problem

#1946 made razer follow a verified main unattended. p620 cannot do the same:
it hosts the four self-hosted nixarchy runners, and a switch restarts their
units, which cancels any job in flight (nixarchy CLAUDE.md §6: two installs
died at one timestamp on 2026-09-13). `scripts/guarded_upgrade.py` therefore
stops at "runner listener drain acknowledgement is not available" and
`hosts/p620/configuration.nix` keeps `features.guarded-upgrade.autoActivate =
false`. So p620, and through its canary razer, only move when someone switches
p620 by hand.

Nothing available closes the gap. GitHub's runner API has no drain or
set-offline endpoint. The nixpkgs runner unit stops with SIGINT, and the
listener's shutdown cancels the active job. A busy-snapshot gate, as today's
`runner_gate()`, races a listener that accepts work right after the snapshot.

## Proposed outcome

p620's guarded upgrade drains its four runners (no new job accepted, an
acquired one finishes normally), keeps them closed through activation and any
rollback, reopens them, and only then publishes its canary. A test in a
disposable repository shows a job at the drain boundary either finishes or
queues through maintenance, and runs exactly once. After it passes,
`autoActivate` is turned on for p620.

## Affected users and systems

p620 (runners, guarded upgrade), razer (follows p620's canary), nixarchy CI.

## Constraints

- No job in flight is ever killed by the upgrade; an ambiguous state defers.
- Runner admission stays closed until the system outcome (commit or rollback)
  is settled, and reopens on every path.
- A carried patch to the pinned actions runner (2.338.2) is re-tested on every
  runner bump.
- Workflow or CI-gate changes in nixarchy are out of scope.

## Open questions

None. The owner chose option 1 (a drain operation in the listener) on
2026-11-27.
