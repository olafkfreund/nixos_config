# Plan: the guards catch every disruptive Justfile recipe

Approved decisions (spec):

- Shared `just` prefix regex, allowing `-f X`, `--justfile X`, `-d X`, `--working-directory X` and `NAME=value`
  words before the recipe:
  `just([[:space:]]+(-f|--justfile|-d|--working-directory)[[:space:]]+[^[:space:]]+|[[:space:]]+[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*=[^[:space:]]*)*[[:space:]]+`.
- Bus hook: also matches `gc`, `cleanup`, `boot-space-clean`, `fix-agenix-remote`, `p510-clear-sessions`, `update`
  (each name followed by whitespace or end).
- p510 guard: also matches `p510-clear-sessions`, and `boot-space-clean p510` / `fix-agenix-remote p510`.
- Coder guard: skips `*=*` in the recipe loop; also blocks `gc`, `cleanup`, `boot-space-clean`,
  `fix-agenix-remote`, `p510-clear-sessions`, `update`, `update-commit`, `build-on-p620`, `_sign-drv`.
- `just update` body becomes `nh os switch --update`. A one-line comment sits above each guarded non-deploy recipe.
