# Spec: a dedicated NVMe for builds and the GitHub runners on p620

## Decisions on the intent's open questions

The owner approved the intent and took the suggested answers:

1. **Missing disk:** the nix daemon falls back to `/`, so p620 can still rebuild itself. The four GitHub runners
   require the mount and do not start without it.
2. **VM workspace:** moves to the new disk. See "The VM workspace keeps its path" for one change to how.
3. **Naming:** a general build mount, `/mnt/build`, labelled `build`.
4. **Old `scratch` SSD:** left unmounted, for a later task.
5. **Runners:** four, unchanged.

## Design

### Disk layout

- **Partition table:** GPT on `/dev/disk/by-id/nvme-Lexar_SSD_EQ790_1TB_QJC054R004014P221Q`, with one partition
  over the whole disk, named `build` (GPT partition label).
- **Filesystem:** ext4 on `…QJC054R004014P221Q-part1`, label `build`, `-m 0` (no root-reserved blocks; nothing on
  it needs them), and default inode density. This matches the other p620 data disks.
- **Mount:** `/mnt/build` by `/dev/disk/by-uuid/<uuid>`, like every other p620 disk; the UUID is read after `mkfs`.
  The label is for people. Options: `noatime nofail x-systemd.device-timeout=10s`, plus
  `x-systemd.before=systemd-tmpfiles-setup.service` and `x-systemd.before=nix-daemon.service`.
  - **Why those two `before` options:** `nofail` drops the mount's `Before=local-fs.target`. Without them, tmpfiles
    and the nix daemon would not wait for the disk at boot. tmpfiles could create the directories on `/` before the
    disk mounts over them, and the daemon could start a build there.
  - **What they do:** order both units after the mount attempt. When the disk is missing, the attempt fails after
    the 10 s timeout, and both units still start, on `/`.
- **TRIM:** weekly `fstrim`, as now. `common-pc-ssd` enables the timer and the disk reports discard support. No
  `discard` mount option, which would add latency to every delete during build churn.
- **Directories,** created on the new filesystem during the cutover and also declared in tmpfiles:
  - `/mnt/build/nix`, `0755 root root`: the nix build-dir;
  - `/mnt/build/vmtest`, `0755 olafkfreund users`: the agents' VM workspace, matching today's `/mnt/data/vmtest`.

### The build-dir

`services.nixarchy-runner.buildDir = "/mnt/build/nix"` in `hosts/p620/configuration.nix`. The module already sets
`nix.settings.build-dir`, the daemon's `TMPDIR`, and a tmpfiles rule from it.

**Fallback:** when the disk is missing, the mount fails, and tmpfiles creates `/mnt/build/nix` on `/`. Builds then
run on the root NVMe, as they did before #2182. The runners can't add 16 GB VM jobs to that, because they don't
start (next section).

### Runners require the disk

`modules/services/nixarchy-runner.nix` sets
`systemd.services."github-runner-nixarchy${suffix}".unitConfig.RequiresMountsFor = [ cfg.buildDir ]` for each
runner. That is a `[Unit]` key, so it can't go in `serviceOverrides`, which only sets `[Service]` keys. This is the
pattern p620's Docker and containerd use for `/mnt/img_pool`.

systemd resolves `/mnt/build/nix` to `Requires=` and `After=mnt-build.mount`:

- a runner waits for the mount attempt;
- if the disk is missing, the runner doesn't start, so CI can't fill `/`;
- on a host whose `buildDir` is on `/`, the setting has no effect.

`systemctl show -p RequiresMountsFor` prints `/mnt/build/nix`.

### The VM workspace keeps its path

nixarchy hardcodes `/mnt/data/vmtest`:

- `AGENTS.md` line 448: agents keep their worktrees there;
- `AGENTS.md` line 788: `flock /mnt/data/vmtest/codex-build.lock`;
- docs and plans.

**Change to the approved intent:** it said the workspace moves and nixarchy's `CLAUDE.md` is updated to match. This
spec moves the data but keeps the path, so nixarchy needs no change. Approving this spec approves that change.

- **The bind mount:** `fileSystems."/mnt/data/vmtest"` with `device = "/mnt/build/vmtest"`, `fsType = "none"`, and
  `bind nofail x-systemd.requires-mounts-for=/mnt/build`, as the existing bind of `/mnt/img_pool/docker/volumes`
  is written. systemd also requires and orders it after `/mnt/data`.
- **Prune:** `services.nixarchy-runner.vmImageDir` stays `/mnt/data/vmtest`, so the prune timer works on the new
  disk. Its `find -xdev` starts at the bind's root, which is on the new filesystem.
- **Prune guard:** the prune service gets `unitConfig.RequiresMountsFor = [ cfg.vmImageDir ]` in the module, plus
  `ConditionPathIsMountPoint = "/mnt/data/vmtest"` on p620. If the bind is missing, the prune doesn't run, and the
  old copy underneath is never pruned.
- **Old data:** the contents of `/mnt/data/vmtest` (4.4 GB, plus worktrees) are copied with `rsync -a -H -A -X`
  during the hold. The originals stay under the mount, hidden, not deleted. Removing them is a later task, with
  the owner's OK.
- **Missing disk:** the bind fails, and `/mnt/data/vmtest` shows the old copy, which is stale but intact. Writes made
  there while the disk is missing diverge from the new copy. When the disk is back, reconcile by hand: list what
  changed with `rsync -n` before remounting. This is documented in a comment next to the bind mount.

### What doesn't change

- `/nix/store` and its `min-free` and `max-free` stay on the root NVMe. Build outputs are still copied into the store
  across filesystems, as they are from `scratch` today.
- The BFQ udev rule (`nvme[0-9]n1`) already covers the new disk, and the daemon's best-effort priority 7 still
  applies. Only its comment, "both NVMe disks", is updated.
- **`scratch`:** its `fileSystems."/var/lib/nix-build"` entry is removed. A switch doesn't unmount it, so the cutover
  does (below). Its data stays on the disk.

## Cutover

The switch changes the build-dir for every build on p620, so it runs under a hold, not just a check.

1. **Build first:** `just test-host p620` from the merged branch, so the switch itself builds nothing.
2. **Hold:** post on the agent bus that p620 builds and `/mnt/data/vmtest` are on hold until "done". Wait until:
   - no nixarchy runner is busy (checked with `gh api` as nixarchy's `AGENTS.md` describes);
   - no `omarchy-edge` verify is running;
   - there are no `nixbld` processes.

   Then stop the four `github-runner-nixarchy*` services, so they can't take a job during the hold.
3. **Check the disk before any write:** the by-id path resolves to the Lexar with serial `QJC054R004014P221Q` and
   1,000,204,886,016 bytes, with no partitions, no signatures (`wipefs -n`, `blkid -p`), not mounted, and with no
   holders.
4. **Partition and check again:** write the GPT table and the partition. Then check that
   `/dev/disk/by-id/…QJC054R004014P221Q-part1` belongs to that disk and has no signature. Then run `mkfs.ext4`
   on `-part1`.
5. **Fill the new filesystem:** mount it temporarily on `/mnt/build`. Create `nix/` and `vmtest/` with the owners and
   modes above. Then run the first `rsync` of `/mnt/data/vmtest`.
6. **Last copy:** re-check that nothing is open under the workspace (`fuser`, `lsof`) and that there are no `nixbld`
   processes. Run the final `rsync` (`--delete`, into the new copy only), then unmount.
7. **Switch:** `just p620`. Then start `mnt-build.mount` and `mnt-data-vmtest.mount` explicitly, because a switch
   doesn't start new `nofail` mounts that nothing requires. Run `systemd-tmpfiles --create`.
8. **Retire `scratch`:** restart `nix-daemon`, so it picks up the new build-dir. Check `/var/lib/nix-build` has no
   holders, then unmount it.
9. **Verify** (below), start the four runners, and post "done" on the bus.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Mount at `/var/lib/nix-build`:** that keeps one path, but it gives the agents' workspace no tidy home on the
  same disk.
- **Rename the workspace to `/mnt/build/vmtest` and edit nixarchy's docs:** this touches another repo, breaks
  agents mid-task, and leaves old paths in history and plans. The bind mount gives the same result with no change
  outside this repo.
- **A symlink from `/mnt/data/vmtest`:** `find -xdev`, `flock` and git worktree paths resolve differently through a
  symlink. A bind mount is the same directory as far as every tool can tell.
- **Mounting by label:** labels can collide if another disk labelled `build` is attached. Every other p620 disk
  mounts by UUID.
- **XFS or btrfs:** these add no benefit for build scratch here, and differ from the rest of p620.
- **`discard` mount option:** it adds latency to every delete during build churn. Weekly `fstrim` is already in
  place.
- **Moving runner state:** 300 MB, and it would reopen the DynamicUser trap the module records.
- **No fallback (required mount):** a dead build disk would then also stop the host from rebuilding itself.
- **More runners:** they are limited by CPU and RAM during VM tests, not by disk.

## Risks

- **Formatting the wrong disk:** the by-id path only, checked before the GPT write and again for `-part1` before
  `mkfs`.
- **A build or agent active during the cutover:** a running build loses its temporary tree, and an agent loses its
  open files under the workspace. Mitigation: the hold, the stopped runners and the checks in steps 2 and 6.
- **A fallback build on `/` fills the root disk:** only p620's own and agents' builds run there. CI is gated, and
  `min-free` still protects the store.
- **A missing disk at boot delays tmpfiles and the daemon** by the 10 s device timeout, as `scratch` delayed its
  mount today.
- **Hidden old data under the bind mount** stays out of sight and keeps using space on `/mnt/data`, until a later
  cleanup.
- **Divergence while the disk is missing:** see "Missing disk" above.

## Verification

- **Before the switch:**
  - `just check-syntax` and `just test-host p620`;
  - `nix eval` shows `nix.settings.build-dir = "/mnt/build/nix"`, `RequiresMountsFor` on all four runners and on
    the prune service, and the two new `fileSystems` entries with their options.
- **After the switch:**
  - `findmnt /mnt/build` and `findmnt /mnt/data/vmtest` show the new filesystem;
  - `nix config show build-dir` shows `/mnt/build/nix`, and `findmnt /var/lib/nix-build` shows nothing;
  - a `nix build --rebuild` of a small derivation creates its tree under `/mnt/build/nix`, seen during the build;
  - a razer build through p620 also builds there;
  - the next nixarchy runner job writes its VM install there, seen as `df` growth on `/mnt/build`;
  - the four runners are active, and `systemctl show -p RequiresMountsFor` lists `/mnt/build/nix`;
  - `lsblk` shows `scratch` unmounted, with its data intact;
  - no failed units.
- **Fallback,** at an approved quiet moment:
  1. stop the runners, unmount the bind and `/mnt/build`, then `systemctl mask --runtime mnt-build.mount`;
  2. starting a runner fails on the mount dependency, the prune service is skipped, and a small `nix build` works
     on `/`;
  3. unmask, mount, start the runners.
